What happens after you paste it

First, it reads the brand. It opens the product page, the about page and the shop, then pulls out the colours, the typefaces, the way the brand talks and the offers it is running. Nothing is invented: every claim it may use is kept with its source.

Then, it studies the competition. An agent opens the public Meta Ad Library, finds the brands you compete with and studies their live image and video ads. It keeps what works, the layouts, hooks and angles, and leaves their branding behind.

  • Us against themSneakers fail, leather boots prevail
  • Problem, then fixOrdinary shoes slip. We conquer.
  • Heritage as proofCrafted for nature since 1992
  • The offerGet 10% off, first order only

Next, it writes the plan. Each ad gets a buyer, a stage of awareness, an angle and its words: the line on the image, the primary text, the headline and the button. Here are three of its ads against wet sneakers, each a different way in.

Finally, it renders. Image ads come out at 4:5 for the feed, videos at 9:16 for Reels and Stories, with your real product photo locked in so the boot in the ad is the boot you sell.
