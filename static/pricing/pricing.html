Frequently Asked Questions

How do creative credits work?

Credits are only deducted when you successfully generate an ad or run campaign intelligence. Image ads use 15–100 credits based on resolution, and video reels use 30–85 credits per second. Failed generations are never billed.

What is included in the Free tier?

You receive 300 credits immediately upon signup with full access to the Image, Video, and Batch Studios. That gives you about 12 high-converting 2K image ads or an 8-second video ad with test images.

What does the Autonomous Campaign Builder do?

Simply paste your brand or product link. The pipeline analyzes your website, extracts brand colors and claims, studies your top competitors' live Meta ads, writes strategic copy angles, and renders 25 image ads and 3 vertical video reels ready for deployment.

Can I upgrade, downgrade, or cancel anytime?

Yes. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your subscription at any time with one click. Upgrades apply immediately with prorated credits.
