Questions

How do credits work?

Every image, video and campaign step spends credits. What it costs depends on the model, the size and, for video, the length: the table above has every price.

What do I get for free?

300 credits once, with the Image, Video and Batch studios. Images use Nano Banana 2.1 up to 2K and videos go up to 720p. That's about 12 image ads, or a short video and a few images.

What is the campaign builder?

Paste one brand link. It reads the brand, studies its competitors' live Meta ads, writes angles and copy, and renders the image and video ads. It comes with Pro and Studio. See a campaign it made.

Can I buy more credits?

Yes, on Starter, Pro and Studio: 1,000 credits for $12 or 5,000 for $55, whenever you need them.

How does yearly billing work?

Pay for the year up front and every plan costs 20% less. Your credits still arrive each month.
