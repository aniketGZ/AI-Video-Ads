# Competitive Creative Intelligence & Category Insights

**Brand:** Arriv | **Category:** personal care / whole-body deodorant cream | **Total Ads Analyzed:** 24

---

## 1. Per Competitor Breakdown
- **Sanfe:** Heavy focus on dark spot removal and direct problem-solving. Uses UGC face-to-camera hooks and split-screen before/after tests.
- **Bombay Shaving Company:** Lifestyle and modern grooming aesthetics. Strong reliance on clean typography overlays and 20–30s demonstration videos.
- **Plum Goodness:** Fragrance, sensory indulgence, and playful lifestyle aesthetics with vibrant pastel color palettes.

---

## 2. Across the Category: Winning Patterns
- **High-Velocity First 2 Seconds:** Top ads immediately establish the bodily problem (sweat, odor, chafing discomfort) before introducing the product bottle.
- **Split-Screen Proof:** Demonstrating immediate texture absorption vs greasy alternatives drives long-running active campaigns.
- **Direct On-Screen Typography:** 3–5 word bold punchy headlines in the top 20% of the 9:16 frame.

---

## 3. Saturated Territory
- Generic bathroom mirror selfie UGC testimonials.
- Cluttered text overlays with discount badges and fake timers.

---

## 4. White Space Opportunities
- **Elevated Editorial Art Direction:** Clean, architectural color-blocked studio settings with premium product hero close-ups.
- **All-Day Commute Realism:** High-speed urban commuting in humid heat — relatable, authentic Gen-Z aesthetic without clinical cringe.
- **Sensory Texture Cinematography:** Slow-motion velvety cream absorption shots on skin with tactile macro lighting.
